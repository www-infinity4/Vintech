## Edition and availability notes

- Sherlock Jr. × Steve Winwood is the current Vintech pairing; the owner reported enjoying the first 8:33. Do not interpret that as a whole-film test.
- Metropolis is the existing Astraflix choice; The Lost World is the Syncord choice. New source validation still matters before reusing a particular print.
- All other shortlist entries are candidates pending exact-source and playback checks. Catalog references are not verified streaming links.
- The General is commonly dated 1926, with its US release in 1927; retain exact release/edition metadata when choosing a copy.
- Use the original silent 1925 Gold Rush, not the later narrated reissue. Sunrise has a silent narrative with a synchronized score/effects; muting changes that presentation.
- Some silent prints include tinting or color sequences. The Phantom of the Opera exists in differing versions; document the selected cut.
- Review historical stereotypes and mature themes when writing audience notes. Review the exact print, restoration and score permissions rather than equating online availability with reuse permission.
